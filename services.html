Rentals: Trackman & HitTrax

Train with the same technology used by college and pro programs. Rent time with our Trackman and HitTrax systems and turn your reps into real, measurable data.

Trackman

Trackman uses Doppler radar to track the full flight of every pitch and batted ball. Instead of guessing, you get precise numbers on exactly how the ball is moving.

What it measures:

  • Pitch velocity, spin rate & spin axis
  • Vertical & horizontal movement (break)
  • Release point & extension
  • Exit velocity, launch angle & distance for hitters

Why it's beneficial: Trackman turns training into a measurable process — you can see how each pitch behaves, fine-tune your arsenal, track progress over time, and build the kind of data profile college coaches and scouts want to see.

HitTrax

HitTrax is a hitting simulator and analytics system that captures the result of every swing and puts it on screen — complete with spray charts and interactive games that make training competitive and fun.

What it measures:

  • Exit velocity & launch angle
  • Projected distance & batted-ball direction
  • Spray charts & hard-hit tendencies
  • Session-over-session progress tracking

Why it's beneficial: HitTrax gives hitters instant feedback on the quality of their contact, keeps kids engaged through gamified sessions, and provides objective data to guide swing adjustments.
