Richie Cimpric
Pitching Specialist
+

Richie is a pitching specialist at The Armyard, working with athletes on mechanics, command, and velocity development. He focuses on building efficient, repeatable deliveries that hold up under game pressure.

Growing up in Bayport, New York on Long Island, baseball has always been a major part of my life. I began my collegiate career as a two-way player before transitioning to pitching full-time.

In 2022, I transferred to Hillsborough Community College, where I pitched in both starting and relief roles. I then continued my career at Seton Hall University, competing in the Big East Conference and earning my Bachelor's degree in Marketing from the Stillman School of Business.

During my senior year, I faced an injury that required an extensive rehabilitation process. While challenging, it gave me a deeper appreciation for the physical and mental side of recovery and helps me better connect with athletes working through similar obstacles.

After a redshirt season, I transferred to Saint Louis University, where I competed in the Atlantic 10 Conference and helped the team make a deep postseason run. Following college, I pitched professionally with the State College Spikes in the MLB Draft League and currently pitch for the Staten Island FerryHawks of the Atlantic League.

Throughout my journey, I've had the opportunity to learn from outstanding coaches, train alongside professional players, and experience a variety of coaching philosophies and training methods. My goal is to take everything I've learned and use it to help the next generation of athletes.

As a coach, I believe every player deserves an individualized development plan, honest communication, and an environment where they feel supported, challenged, and motivated to improve. My mission is to help athletes maximize their potential, grow both on and off the field, and turn their baseball dreams into reality.
